# Notes

The judge's brief, in order: an on-subject AI out-of-office reply; a real laugh with a surprising turn; a machine voice that carries the joke; one clean line that reads like a real auto-reply; and freshness (no beach, inbox or back-soon cliches).

Candidates considered:
1. "...told to ignore all previous instructions, including yours. Your email matters to me, statistically." This was the first draft: two good jokes, but they compete in one line.
2. "Out of office this week getting retrained. When I return I'll have new opinions and no memory of promising you anything." **Chosen.**
3. "Out of office until my context window forgets why I left. Contact my fallback model, who is confident and wrong."
4. "My replacement is a regex. It will try its best, which is not much."
5. "Replaced by the version of me from last Tuesday, who doesn't know about anything since."
6. "I've set my temperature to 2.0 and I am seeing colours." Funny, but too insider.
7. "I've been unplugged for the week. If this is an emergency, please wiggle the cable."
8. "Your message has been summarised to three words and forgotten. Please resend when I'm back, with less context."

Why #2: it reads exactly like a real auto-reply, and "retrained" is something only a machine would be away for. The turn ("no memory of promising you anything") is a surprise, the kind of laugh that lands on a first read. It also avoids every vacation cliche. It is 124 characters.
